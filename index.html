❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

What is Wonder Hub? ▾
Wonder Hub is a premium Telegram-based media sharing community. It allows users to share photos, videos, and GIFs in a secure, moderated environment with real-time delivery to all members.
How do I get started? ▾
Simply click the "Start Bot" button, follow the verification process, and send 5 media files to complete your registration. Once approved, you'll gain full access to the community.
Is Wonder Hub safe to use? ▾
Absolutely! Wonder Hub employs strict moderation, content guidelines, and admin oversight to ensure a safe and respectful environment. We have zero tolerance for illegal or harmful content.
What are the community rules? ▾
Our key rules include: no illegal content, only 16+ material allowed, no spam or flooding, no external links, and respect for all members. Violations result in a permanent ban.
